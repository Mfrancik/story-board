Mockup gallery: three directions

What you asked for: "for any mockup, i have a mockup gallery button i go to, and that gives me a full screen view with a small title and description, tells me where, shows me a current version (if that exists), then i can see the two mockups side by side in a single screen." All three put a Mockups link in the design-A sidebar with the number waiting for a pick, and each adds a second way in from somewhere you already are. They use the real sets: story-board's SB-3 to SB-18 (picked, with the reason from each story's gate) plus SB-21 itself, and coins' newest seven of 263. VAR-19 and SCOPE-7 are awaiting a pick, SCOPE-9 is a new page with no current version, and SCOPE-5 was "neither". The option panes load the real option files. coins' files load from ~/Code/coins beside this repo. Current is a drawn grey stand-in for the screenshot the build would capture. The dark strip at the top of each option switches between its states. Picking is visual only: it shows the toast SB-13 would send.

Option A: grid + lightbox

A /mockups page of cards with live thumbnails, grouped by project, with sets awaiting a pick first. Filters for status and project, and search. A card opens a full-screen lightbox: title, description and Where on top, then Current / A / B / C tabs and a width switch. Side by side splits the lightbox into two panes, each with its own picker and a swap. The pick buttons sit along the bottom. You can also get in from the story modal's new "Open in mockup gallery".

Good: the closest match to what you described: gallery, then full screen, then compare. You can spot a set by sight, and ← → steps through every set without closing the lightbox.

Costs: one live iframe per card is heavy at coins' 263 sets, so it needs paging (only the newest are shown). The thumbnails are small at 1280 scale, so they tell sets apart more than they show detail.

Option B: library + stage

A list with no thumbnails (ID, title, the path and A B C chips, the picked one filled) sits beside a stage. The stage has a toolbar: tabs for Current, each option and Compare, plus width and Full screen, which hides the sidebar and the list. Compare splits the stage and adds swap and "scroll together". The pick sits in an amber bar under the stage. J / K moves through the list. You can also get in from the story page's "Open in Mockups".

Good: scales to 263 sets because text rows are cheap, and only one or two frames are ever loaded. The fastest way to work down a queue of picks. Everything, including the reason, stays on one screen.

Costs: nothing visual until you pick a row. Full screen is a step rather than the default. On a phone it is two screens, the list and then the stage.

Option C: filmstrip + slider

Each set is a row with a filmstrip: Current first, then every option. Present opens an edge-to-edge presentation. You swipe or press ← → between versions, press ↑ ↓ between stories, and a thumbnail strip runs along the bottom. Slider lays one version over another with a divider you drag. Side by side is there when you want both whole. "Present the 3 awaiting a pick" runs a queue. You can also get in from a Home "Mockups to pick" card.

Good: the slider shows exactly what changed from today's page to an option, in one frame. Before and after sit in every row. Swiping works well on a phone.

Costs: the slider only reads well when both versions share a layout. Two different directions just look cut in half, which is why side by side is there too. A row per set makes a long page. To click inside a page you turn on "Click inside the page", because swipe owns the pointer.

The same in every option

  • Each set shows one status, always in words: Awaiting pick (amber), Picked A/B/C with the reason from the story's gate, or Neither · parked.
  • Where is the route plus a plain note ("/p/coins/preflight (Preflight tab)", "/mockups (new page)"). A set with no page yet shows "No current version — new page" instead of Current.
  • Frames render a page at 1280, 768 or 375 and scale it to fit. They never enlarge, so a phone-width page sits centred at real size. "Open file ↗" opens the raw option file.
  • Picking follows SB-13: the board sends the pick and your one-line reason to the session that owns the story, and never writes the repo itself. "Neither" parks the set.
  • Scope note: the draft SB-21 story leaves out the Current pane (that is SB-23, fed by SB-22's journey shots) and picking from the board (picks stay in /story and /build for now). Both are drawn here so you can see the whole flow. If they stay out, the Current tab and the pick bar are the pieces that come off, and the layouts do not change.
  • Worth deciding at build time: where the Current screenshot comes from. It could be a headless capture of the live route when the set is read, or an upload. Sets whose files do not follow option-x.html (COIN-11's rounds, VIEW-35's sticker files) show the extras as "+ other files".
