      Open "didn't meet your rules" and see them

      Story

      As the owner, when a coin lands in "didn't meet your rules", I want to open that list and see which rule each coin failed, so that I can loosen a rule or accept the miss without guessing.

      Why

      Today the count shows on the acquisition page but the list is a dead end. Owners re-run searches by hand to work out which rule filtered a coin out.

      In scope

      • The "didn't meet your rules" count becomes a link to the filtered list.
      • Each row names the first rule the coin failed and the value that failed it.
      • Rules link back to the rule editor.

      Acceptance criteria

      • Given 4 coins failed my rules, when I click the count, then I see those 4 coins and no others.
      • Given a coin failed the max-price rule, when I view its row, then it reads "Price $412 is over your $350 limit".
      • Given a coin failed two rules, when I view its row, then the first failing rule shows and "+1 more" expands the rest.
      • Given no coins failed, when I open the list, then I see "Every coin met your rules."

      Test plan

      • Feature test: the list is scoped to failed coins for the signed-in owner.
      • Unit test: the rule explainer formats each rule type.
      • Browser check at 375 and 1280.
