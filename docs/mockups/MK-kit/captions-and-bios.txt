- TrueView Tuesday: TrueView Tuesday. Show us your best toner.

Did you know
- What "BN, RB, RD" means: On copper, BN is brown, RB is red-brown, RD is full red. Red is rarest, and it shows in the price.
- Key dates: A key date is the hardest coin to find in a series. For Lincoln cents, think 1909-S VDB.
- Mint marks: That tiny letter matters. D is Denver, S is San Francisco, O is New Orleans, CC is Carson City.
- What CAC means: A green CAC sticker means a second set of eyes agreed the coin is solid for its grade.
- Proof vs. business strike: Proofs were struck twice on polished dies for collectors. That's why they shine like mirrors.
