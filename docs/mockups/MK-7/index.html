Setting up a campaign, simplified: TrueView Tuesday

You asked for it to be “simple and easy to manage and use and intuitive, not super complicated, but still needs to do everything we need”. A campaign now has only five things: a name, the weekly day(s) and time with an on/off switch, topics (ideas ticked from the kit's idea menu plus your own topics in your own words, each with optional detail), a one-or-two-sentence brief, and photos (library photo, text only, or let the AI decide). Prepare-ahead is no longer a setting: it is always the next 2 weeks, and the Fill next 2 weeks button in the calendar tray makes the drafts when you want them. All three options use the same data and the calendar from MK-10 option A. Each one shows the campaign list with on/off switches, the self-writing sentence, the Next posts preview, adding your own topics, and the dashed ghosts that land on the calendar when you save. Every page works at 375px.

v2: simplified after the feasibility review; v1 kept in v1/. Gone from v1: photo tags and the 90-day reuse rule, the low-photo warning, start and end dates, every-other-week, prepare-ahead weeks, the starter pack, templates and the data-source box. What's left of that is one quiet line under Photos: “Coins photos: coming later”. The Marketing sub-nav is now Calendar · Review · Library · Settings, and campaigns live in the Calendar tray.

New in all three: Upcoming posts. You asked to “see the next several posts of that one specifically in a modal I can pop up and view easily”. Every campaign now has an Upcoming posts button: in the tray list, inside the campaign form (next to Next posts), and on that campaign's dashed ghosts on the calendar. It opens one modal with the campaign sentence and its on/off switch on top, then the next 6 dates in order. Each row shows its state: confirmed (thumbnail, caption, Copy caption and Download image), 3 drafts to review (Review link), open (a Fill button for that one date), or missed for the last past date when it didn't go out. Esc or a click on the backdrop closes it. Set spotlight and Ask the community show every state; TrueView Tuesday is new, so its dates start open.

AOne plain page the sentence on top, four short sections

Clicking a campaign in the Calendar tray, or + New campaign, opens a normal page. The sentence sits on top and rewrites itself as you type. Below it are four numbered sections: 1 Name and when, 2 Topics (your own words first, then the idea menu grouped by category), 3 Brief / voice, 4 Photos. Next posts is pinned on the right. Create campaign takes you back to the Calendar with the new ghosts pulsing. This is the most obvious of the three: nothing is hidden, nothing to learn, and it follows the canonical “dedicated page” pattern.

A modal over the calendar, with a big editable sentence: “Every Tuesday at 7pm, post about toned coins and TrueView, plus 2 topics in your own words, with a photo from the library.” and “Sounds like: “…””. Gaps and questions

  • Where campaigns live. With no Campaigns sub-nav item, the list is the Calendar tray (all three options). Settings could carry a second link to it. Is the tray enough?
  • Fill next 2 weeks. It covers every campaign that is on, and only open dates (no post, no drafts yet). The mockups make 3 drafts per date. Should Create also offer “Fill these now”, or keep filling as one deliberate button?
  • Topic rotation. The Next posts preview assumes the AI takes turns: your own topic, then a menu idea, and so on. Should your own words get more weight, or should the AI pick freely from the pool?
  • Menu ideas with placeholders. Ideas like “Slot filled” or “Key date spotlight” need [set] or [key date] data that won't exist until the phase-3 coins API. Hide them for now, or let the AI write around them?
  • Changing a day. Posts that are already scheduled or have drafts keep their own date; only the ghosts move. Pausing a campaign removes its ghosts and leaves its drafts in Review.
  • Copy rules and taste. No longer shown in the form. They apply to every campaign, as warnings and as like/dislike examples; Settings and Review own them.
  • Upcoming posts modal. A per-date Fill only fills that date. A missed row offers “Post it now”, which opens the post to copy and post by hand; the Mac-asleep rule (mark missed, never send late) still needs confirming. Should the modal show more than 6 dates, or a “Show more”?
  • Editing other campaigns. Only TrueView Tuesday opens in these mockups; the others say they open in the same form. Create and edit share one form, as the reuse rule requires.
