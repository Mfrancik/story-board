== IDEA MENU ==

Sets
- What are you building?: What set are you working on?
- Slot filled: Another slot filled. [coin] joins the [set] set. [x] of [y] done.
- Type set 101: A type set is one of each design, not every date. It's the best way to own a piece of every chapter.

Toned & TrueView
- TrueView Tuesday: TrueView Tuesday. Show us your best toner.
- Toning 101: Toning is chemistry, not damage. Natural color can add value. Cleaning takes it away.

Show & tell
- Coin of the week: Coin of the week: [coin], [grade]. What do you see first, the strike or the color?

Did you know
- What "BN, RB, RD" means: On copper, BN is brown, RB is red-brown, RD is full red. Red is rarest, and it shows in the price.
- Mint marks: That tiny letter matters. D is Denver, S is San Francisco, O is New Orleans, CC is Carson City.

Ask the community
- Rarity or condition?: Same price. Rare and worn, or common and near perfect?
- Grade it: No peeking at the label. What grade would you give this one?
