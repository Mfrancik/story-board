The Review page at /p/coins/marketing/review

You asked for a page where you go through the upcoming posts, choose between generated designs, and see when each one is planned to post. Nothing goes out unless you picked it. Each option sits in the shell under a new Marketing tab, with the sub-nav Library · Review · Calendar · Designer · Settings. All three use the same mock data. The mock clock is Mon 12 Oct 11:20. The schedule has four recurring slots: Coin of the week Tue 10:00, TrueView Tuesday Tue 19:00, Set spotlight Fri 18:00 and Ask the community Sun 12:00. Yesterday's Sunday post was missed because the Mac was asleep. Captions come from the kit's real copy and idea menu. Candidate 4 for Tue 13 Oct (1923 Peace dollar) breaks the copy rules on purpose, and the Sun 18 Oct comparison needs a price source before it can be confirmed. All three options also have like / dislike on every candidate and every design, with a "Your taste" hint. Every page is interactive and works at 375px.

A large post preview has ← / → flip buttons, design-variant pills under it, and a filmstrip of 3–4 candidate thumbnails. After Confirm, the slot turns into a green "Posts … to Facebook" card. J / K move to the previous or next slot. Optimises for care: one decision at a time with the most room for the post itself. It is the closest to how the post will look in the feed.

BKeep or pass triage one deck of every candidate

Every candidate for every slot that needs review goes into one deck, 13 cards across four slots, in time order. Press ← to pass or → to keep, drag the card on touch, and press Z to undo. Keep and pass are the like and dislike signal, so after a pass you get optional reason chips. Each design also has its own thumbs. Kept cards collect in a column on the right, grouped by slot. There you pick one per slot and press Confirm. Keeping only shortlists a card and never schedules it. If you pass on every candidate for a slot, the slot offers Regenerate. Optimises for speed: clearing a week of options in one sitting, with the decision on each card taking a single key.

Gaps and questions

  • Likes and dislikes need a store and a consumer. Each thumb is a row: candidate or variant, up or down, an optional reason, and when. The generator reads the totals as a soft preference for content (which coins, sets or topics), template choice and caption style (length, tone). It never varies the brand: the swirl logo with StackerMint and BETA, tan #B79F82 and ink #222, Instrument Sans and 5px corners are fixed inputs to every template. The lean chips in "Your taste" (for example "+ set progress", "− too wordy") need a summariser. Is a simple count by template and reason enough, or should it be a short line the AI writes? A like never confirms; Confirm stays separate in all three options.
  • Is a dislike also a reject? In A and C, a dislike leaves the candidate where it is. In B, a pass removes it from the deck. Should a disliked candidate be hidden from the slot in A and C as well?
  • Should copy rules block or warn? These mockups block Confirm until every rule passes. The alternative is "Confirm anyway" with the warnings saved alongside the post. Some rules can be checked by a script (length, filler words, cert claims). "Similar value + price source" needs the price source as a field on comparison templates, which the Sun 18 Oct card shows.
  • Missed means the sender's clock passed while the Mac was asleep. The queue has to record a post's due time and its attempt time. If the attempt comes more than N minutes after the due time, the post becomes missed instead of being sent. What should N be: 5 minutes? 15? Reschedule defaults to the next day at the same hour.
  • When should candidates be generated? The mock generates them about four days ahead ("Generates Thu 15 Oct"), and Generate now forces it. Regenerate replaces the candidates you haven't liked. Should liked candidates be kept when you regenerate?
  • Confirmed posts are locked to one design. Edit in designer opens Designer (MK-10) with that candidate and design loaded. Saving from there should come back here as a new variant, not overwrite the original.
  • Facebook only for now. "Posts … to Facebook" assumes one connected page per project. More channels would turn Confirm into a choice of channels.
