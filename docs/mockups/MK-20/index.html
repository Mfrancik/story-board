What every option shows

  • The sub-nav gains Review with a count badge (8 dates; it drops as you keep, and hides when the queue is empty). Campaign chips: All + each campaign with a count; the heading count follows the filter.
  • Per ad: Keep (MK-9 ApprovePost; the other two are discarded and the date leaves the queue), 👍, 👎 with too wordy · wrong tone · boring · off-brand (RecordFeedback), and open in studio.
  • Per date: Reject all 3 opens a confirm with one optional shared reason, then the date shows Drafting… (Tue 20 Oct 19:00 TrueView Tuesday starts in that state). When the new three arrive they show Rendering… and then a "New draft" tag.
  • States: Tue 20 Oct ad 3 is Rendering… (Keep is disabled until it lands). Fri 16 Oct ad 2 failed to render, so it shows MK-5's HTML text-card fallback (post-preview) with Retry. The empty state reads "Nothing to pick. Fill some dates on a campaign page."
  • Keyboard: ←/→ dates, 1/2/3 keep, R reject all (Enter confirms, Esc cancels). After a Keep, focus stays in the same place and the next date moves into it.
