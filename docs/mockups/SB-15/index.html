Stories by initiative: three layouts

All three sit inside the design-A shell from SB-7, under the new tab bar (Dashboard | Handbook | Stories). They show /p/coins/stories with real data: all 946 on-ref coins stories in 57 initiatives, read from the dev DB on 2026-09-29. Sections are in SB-10's order (most open work first), and stories are in natural ID order. Each page has working filter chips (Built, To do, Draft, Cancelled, Other), Expand all / Collapse all, and a note when you click a story; in the build, that click opens the SB-8 modal. The dark strip at the top switches between coins, the first-load skeleton and the empty state. A dashed "Other project: rent-track" panel at the bottom shows the grey tags (done, in, "no status").

Things to try: open branding (7 built, 3 draft), open acquisition (ACQ-22, 23 and 24 are struck through), open mobile (83 stories), find import (the parked initiative), then turn Built off.

Option A: accordion list

One slim row per initiative, with a chevron, the name, a "parked" tag, an inline mini status bar, the per-status counts and the total. A row opens in place into a compact ID | title | tag list, and its header sticks while you scroll a long section such as mobile. Several sections can be open at once.

Good: closest to the story's wording ("openable sections"), and it follows SB-10's Progress by initiative row design, so the two pages read alike. At coins' scale, 57 rows fit in about 2,300 px, and the bars make "what's left" easy to scan.

Costs: 39 of the 57 initiatives have no open work, so the long tail is a lot of green. With Expand all, the page is 946 rows long.

Option B: card grid

Each initiative is a card with a donut ring split by status, the percentage built in the middle, and the counts below. Opening a card puts a full-width panel under that card's row. The panel lists the stories in two columns on wide screens. The grid has 4 columns on a very wide screen, 3 on desktop, 2 on a tablet and 1 on a phone.

Good: the most visual of the three. The rings show which initiatives are nearly done at a glance, and an open panel has room for long titles.

Costs: at 57 cards, it is the tallest of the three (19 rows at 3 across, 57 cards stacked on a phone). The order reads across and then down, which follows SB-10's ranking less clearly than a list. Expand all puts a panel under every row, which gets noisy. The ring is a new visual element that no other page uses.

Option C: dense two-pane

On the left is a sticky, scrollable list of all 57 initiatives. Each shows its open / total count (e.g. payments 20 / 24) and a hairline status bar. The chosen initiative fills the right pane with a header bar, its counts and its stories. Expand all switches the right pane to one long list grouped by initiative, with sticky group headers, and the left list jumps to a group. On a phone, the left list becomes a select.

Good: handles coins' scale best. All 57 initiatives stay visible in one scroll area, and moving between them takes one click with no jump in the page. The open / all numbers put open work first, like SB-10.

Costs: only one section is open at a time unless you use Expand all. That fits "open branding" less literally than an accordion does. The sections do not collapse on their own, so Expand all / Collapse all switches between two views. On a phone, the select hides the overview of all initiatives.

The trade-off at coins' scale

coins has many initiatives, and most of them are finished. 39 of 57 have no open work, and 844 of 946 stories are built. A shows the most on one screen and is the most literal match for the story. B looks the best but grows the tallest. C is the fastest to move around in, but it swaps "open several sections" for "choose one". For a small project such as rent-track (1 initiative) or asset-track (4), all three work well. For rent-track, C's left pane would be almost empty.

The same in every option

  • Tag colours reuse the status tokens: built emerald, to do (approved) sky, draft amber. Cancelled is a new muted variant: an outlined tag, with the ID and title struck through. Any other value gets a grey tag showing its raw text. The danger tone on Home and on story rows is unchanged.
  • Filtering never changes a section's counts. When filters hide part of an open section, it says "N hidden by the filters", and a section with every row hidden disappears. "24 of 57 initiatives" is shown next to Expand all.
  • Everything is Alpine. The whole story list is rendered once, so opening a section or changing a filter never makes a server call.
