MOB-56 — A bullion room built for the phone

Story

As a collector on the phone, I want a bullion room designed for a phone, so that I can see what I hold and what it's worth at a glance.

Why

The room is MOB-35's "vault": a fixed count-and-melt row over a two-up grid of VaultCards, one per line, from GET /v1/bullion/summary. The owner wants it redesigned. The web /bullion page is a reference, not a template.

In scope

  • Two or three phone-native directions at the mockup gate. At least one should explore each of:
    • a metal-first summary: totals per metal (oz and melt) as the top band, lines grouped under their metal
    • a list with product pictures (BUL-65/66's product images) and the quantity and container line ("7 tubes of 20")
    • the owner's pick of the web desk's strongest idea, adapted to one column
  • Build the chosen direction, fed by GET /v1/bullion/summary.
  • Keep the room's four states (loading, empty invite, load error with retry, 403 draws nothing) and its logging, as MOB-35 ruled.

Out of scope (do NOT build)

  • Adding or editing bullion (the capture flow).
  • Spot prices' source or the spot rail.
  • The web /bullion page.

Acceptance criteria

  • Given a summary with gold and silver lines, when the room renders, then every line appears once, with its quantity in the entered unit and its value.
  • Given a line with a product image, when it renders, then the image shows. Given none, or a failed load, then the placeholder shows.
  • Given an empty summary, when the room renders, then the first-bullion invite shows.
  • D: given the room opens, the ounces-and-spot strip is collapsed to one row; tapping it opens the per-metal ozt + spot in place.
