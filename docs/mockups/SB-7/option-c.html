        Story

        As the owner, when listings are hidden because they didn’t meet my acquisition rules, I want to open that count and see which rule each one failed, so I can tune my rules instead of guessing.

        Why

        Today “didn’t meet your rules: 14” is a dead end. Owners loosen rules blindly and then get flooded with listings they would never buy.

        In scope

        • The “didn’t meet your rules” count becomes a link that opens a panel.
        • The panel lists each hidden listing with the rule or rules it failed.
        • Each failed rule links to its setting in acquisition settings.
        • Out of scope: editing rules inside the panel.

        Acceptance criteria

        • Given 3 listings failed the max price rule, when I open “didn’t meet your rules”, then I see 3 rows, each labelled “Over max price”.
        • Given a listing failed two rules, when I open the panel, then both rules are listed on its row.
        • Given no listings failed, when I view acquisition, then the count reads 0 and is not a link.
        • Given the panel is open, when I click a failed rule, then I land on that rule’s setting.

        Test plan

        • Pest feature test: the panel query returns each hidden listing with its failed rules.
        • Pest feature test: zero failures renders a plain count, no link.
        • Browser check at 375 and 1280: panel opens, rule links land on the right setting.
